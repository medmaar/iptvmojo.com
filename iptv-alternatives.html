Switching Providers — Common Questions

I'm looking at a specific small IPTV provider I found online — how do I know if it's reliable? +
Look for four things regardless of the brand name: a free trial (so you can test before paying), transparent pricing with no hidden renewal fees, real device compatibility with what you own, and responsive support you can reach before you buy, not just after.
Why are there so many similarly-named IPTV services? +
The IPTV reseller market is highly fragmented — many small operators resell access to similar underlying server infrastructure under their own brand name, which is why you'll see dozens of similar-sounding names (adjective + "IPTV") with comparable offerings advertised at similar prices.
Is it safe to switch providers if my current one is unreliable? +
Yes — IPTV subscriptions are typically prepaid with no contract, so you can simply let your current plan run out and start fresh with a new provider. There's no cancellation process tying you to the old service.
Will I need new hardware if I switch to IPTVmojo? +
