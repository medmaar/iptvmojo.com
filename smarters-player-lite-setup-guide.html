Setup

Set Up Smarters Player Lite

1

Install from the App Store

Search Smarters Player Lite on your iPhone, iPad or Apple TV and install it. It's free.

2

Accept the terms and choose a login type

Pick Login with Xtream Codes API. M3U also works but loads more slowly and lacks catch-up.

3

Enter your IPTVmojo details

Any name, then the username, password and server URL (including http:// and port if shown) from your welcome email.

4

Wait for the first load

Live TV, movies, series and the guide download. The first load takes a minute with a large lineup.

5

Start watching

Open Live TV, pick a category and a channel. Use the guide button for the EPG and the clock icon for catch-up.

Smarters Player Lite vs IPTV Smarters Pro

The Smarters developer publishes the app under different names on different stores. On Android, Fire TV and Windows it's IPTV Smarters Pro; on Apple's App Store, it's Smarters Player Lite. For watching IPTVmojo, both offer the same essentials: Xtream Codes login, live TV with EPG, movies and series, catch-up and multiple profiles. Interface details differ slightly between platforms, and the developer occasionally offers optional paid extras inside the app. None of them are needed to watch IPTVmojo.

AirPlay vs running it on Apple TV

You can AirPlay a channel from your iPhone to an Apple TV or AirPlay-capable smart TV, but the phone has to stay on the same Wi-Fi and awake. For full matches, install Smarters Player Lite on the Apple TV itself and log in there. It uses the same single connection while streaming. More options in our Apple TV & iPhone guide.

Fixes for common errors

  • "Invalid username or password": details are case-sensitive, and the iPhone keyboard auto-capitalises the first letter. Check it.
  • Server URL rejected: include http:// and the port number exactly as in your email.
  • Black screen on some channels: in Settings → Player, switch between the default and alternative player.
  • Guide empty: use Settings → Refresh EPG and wait a minute.
  • Buffering: see fix IPTV buffering.

Alternatives on Apple devices

GSE Smart IPTV is a capable free alternative, and iPlayTV is a paid app with a very Apple-like guide. TiviMate isn't available on Apple devices. See the full list in our IPTV player guide.

Smarters Player Lite Questions

Is Smarters Player Lite free? +
Yes. It's free on the App Store. You only pay for your IPTV subscription.
Is Smarters Player Lite the same as IPTV Smarters Pro? +
It's the Apple App Store version from the same developer, with the same Xtream Codes login and core features.
Does Smarters Player Lite work on Apple TV? +
Yes. Install it from the App Store on Apple TV 4K or Apple TV HD and log in with your IPTVmojo details.
Does Smarters Player Lite have catch-up? +
Yes. Channels with catch-up show a clock icon, and IPTVmojo offers up to 7 days on supported channels.
Why can't I find IPTV Smarters Pro on the App Store? +
On Apple devices the app is listed as Smarters Player Lite. Search for that name.
