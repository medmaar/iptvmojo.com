IPTV on Enigma2 & Satellite Receivers

Already have a satellite receiver? Many can show IPTV channels alongside satellite ones, in the same guide, with the same remote. Here's how, depending on what your box runs.

Setup

Enigma2: Add IPTVmojo with XStreamity

1

Update your image

Open Menu → Setup → Software update so the plugin feed is current. OpenATV, OpenPLi and similar images all work.

2

Install XStreamity

Go to Menu → Plugins → Download plugins (or the green button in the plugin browser), find XStreamity under Extensions and install it. Restart the GUI when asked.

3

Add your Xtream login

Open XStreamity → Add Playlist. Enter the server URL (with port, if your email shows one), username and password from IPTVmojo.

4

Load Live, VOD and Series

XStreamity downloads categories and guide data. Browse Live TV, Movies or Series. Catch-up channels show a rewind option.

5

Optional: make bouquets

XStreamity's bouquet maker can write selected IPTV categories into your main channel list, so they sit next to your satellite channels.

The M3U bouquet method (no plugin)

If you only want live channels in your main list, you can convert the IPTVmojo M3U into an Enigma2 bouquet. Tools such as e2m3u2bouquet (available as a plugin on most images) read your M3U link, build bouquets by category, and can refresh them on a schedule along with the guide. It's a good fit if you use the receiver mostly for live TV and want IPTV channels to zap exactly like satellite ones. For movies and series, XStreamity is better.

Tips for Enigma2 owners

  • Use the right stream type. If channels open but stay black, switch the service type in the plugin settings (for example between the default player and exteplayer3/gstreamer). Some images decode HEVC/4K better with one or the other.
  • Keep bouquets lean. Importing all 50,000+ channels slows any receiver. Pick the categories you watch.
  • Back up first. Make a settings backup before adding plugins, so you can roll back if an image update conflicts.
  • Older SD/HD receivers may not decode modern H.265 streams. A Firestick on another HDMI input is often the fix.

Dreamlink, Amiko, Xsarius, TVIP and other receivers

These brands make several different families of box, so the right method depends on the exact model. Dreamlink's T-series are Android-based boxes with Dreamlink's own app, and they also take TiviMate or Smarters. Amiko and Xsarius have offered both Linux (including Enigma2) and Android models over the years. TVIP and similar boxes use a built-in IPTV/portal menu. Check About in your box's menu, match it to the table above, and if in doubt, send us the model on WhatsApp. For buying advice, see do you need an IPTV box?

FAQ

Enigma2 & Receiver Questions

Can I watch IPTV on my satellite receiver? +
Yes, if it runs Enigma2 or Android, or has a built-in IPTV/portal menu. Enigma2 receivers use plugins like XStreamity; Android receivers use normal IPTV apps.
What is the best IPTV plugin for Enigma2? +
XStreamity is the most popular for Xtream Codes logins because it handles live TV, VOD, series and catch-up. For live channels in your main list, an M3U-to-bouquet plugin works well.
Do IPTV channels show in the same EPG as satellite? +
With bouquet tools, yes. IPTV categories become normal bouquets with guide data, so you can zap between satellite and IPTV channels.
Why are IPTV channels black on my Enigma2 box? +
Usually a codec or player issue. Change the service type or player in the plugin settings, and confirm your receiver can decode H.265/HEVC for 4K channels.
Does IPTVmojo support the Portal (MAC) login for receivers? +
Yes. Alongside Xtream Codes and M3U, IPTVmojo supports Portal logins for MAG-style and Stalker-compatible devices.
