What Is IPTV? A Complete Beginner's Guide (2026)

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) is a way of delivering TV channels over a regular internet connection instead of through a cable line, satellite dish, or over-the-air antenna. Instead of your TV signal traveling through dedicated broadcast infrastructure, it's sent as data packets over the internet — the same basic technology that streams a YouTube video or a Netflix show, just applied to live TV channels.

How IPTV Actually Works

When you watch IPTV, your streaming app (like TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro) connects to a provider's server and requests a specific channel. The server sends that channel's video as a continuous stream of data over the internet, and your app decodes it and displays it in real time. This is fundamentally different from cable, which broadcasts every channel simultaneously down a physical wire whether you're watching or not.

IPTV essentially fills the gap between the two: it gives you live, linear TV channels (like cable) but delivered over the internet (like Netflix), and most providers — including IPTVmojo — bundle in a large video-on-demand library of movies and series as well.

What Do You Need to Use IPTV?

  • A stable internet connection — 15–25 Mbps is enough for smooth HD/4K streaming.
  • A device — a smartphone, smart TV, computer, Firestick, or Android box. See our full device guide.
  • An IPTV app — such as TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro, which displays the channels.
  • A subscription — login credentials from a provider like IPTVmojo that give the app access to channels.

Is IPTV Legal?

Using an IPTV app itself is not illegal — apps like TiviMate and VLC are just media players. Whether a specific subscription is fully licensed for every channel it offers is a more complex question that varies by provider and region. See our dedicated guide on is IPTV legal for a fuller answer.

Why People Switch to IPTV

The main draws are cost and flexibility: no long-term contract, no installation appointment, and typically a much larger channel selection (including international and niche sports channels) than a standard cable package. The trade-off is that stream quality depends entirely on your internet connection and the provider's server reliability, rather than dedicated broadcast infrastructure.

Do I need special equipment to use IPTV? +
No special equipment is required — a phone, smart TV, computer, or an inexpensive Firestick is enough, along with a free IPTV app and a subscription.
Is IPTV the same as Kodi? +
No. Kodi is an open-source media center application that can play many types of content, including IPTV streams via an add-on. IPTV itself is the underlying technology/service, not a specific app.
Can I watch IPTV on more than one TV at once? +
Yes, as long as your subscription plan includes enough simultaneous connections — IPTVmojo offers plans from 1 to 10 connections.
Does IPTV include on-demand movies, or just live channels? +
Most modern IPTV subscriptions, including IPTVmojo, include both: live channels and a large video-on-demand (VOD) library of movies and series.
