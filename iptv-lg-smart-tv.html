IPTV on LG Smart TV (webOS)

Every LG smart TV sold in the last several years runs webOS, and webOS has its own short list of IPTV apps. Here is which ones work, how to set them up, and what to do when your LG model is too old.

Quick answer: On an LG TV you can't install TiviMate or sideload Android apps. Install Smart IPTV, IBO Player or SS IPTV from the LG Content Store, then load your IPTVmojo playlist through the app's website using the MAC address shown on screen. IBO Player is included free with the IPTVmojo 1-year plan. If your TV is older than about 2017, a $30–$50 streaming stick is the easier route.

Setup

Set Up IPTV on LG in 5 Steps

1

Open the LG Content Store

Press the Home button on the LG Magic Remote and open the LG Content Store (called Apps on newer webOS versions).

2

Install an IPTV player

Search for Smart IPTV, IBO Player or SS IPTV and install one. App availability varies by country and TV year, so use whichever appears.

3

Note the MAC address

Open the app. It shows a MAC address (and, for IBO Player, a device key). Write both down exactly as shown.

4

Upload your IPTVmojo playlist

On your phone or computer, open the app's official website, enter the MAC address and paste the M3U link or Xtream details from your IPTVmojo welcome email.

5

Restart the app

Close the app completely and reopen it. Channels, the EPG guide and VOD categories load on the TV.

Which LG app should you pick?

If you are on the IPTVmojo 1-year plan, start with IBO Player: the activation is already covered, the interface is closer to TiviMate than the other webOS apps, and it handles live TV, movies and series in separate sections. On shorter plans, SS IPTV costs nothing and is fine for live channels, while Smart IPTV is the most established option and the one most LG owners already know. Our MAC-address app guide walks through each website step by step.

All three are players, not channel providers. They show nothing until you add a subscription, which is why you will need your IPTVmojo credentials (or a free 24-hour trial) before setup.

Check your webOS version first

Open Settings → All Settings → General (or Support) → About This TV / TV Information. The label moved around between model years, but the screen always lists the webOS version.

  • webOS 4.0 and newer (2018+): all of the apps above install normally.
  • webOS 3.x (2016–2017): Smart IPTV and SS IPTV usually work; newer apps may be missing from the store.
  • NetCast or webOS 1–2 (2015 and older): the store is effectively frozen. Plug in a Fire TV Stick or Google TV device instead. You get TiviMate, better performance and continued app updates for the price of a couple of months of cable.

LG vs Samsung for IPTV

LG webOS and Samsung Tizen behave similarly for IPTV: both rely on MAC-activated players and neither allows sideloading. The practical difference is the app list. IBO Player and SS IPTV are easy to find on both, while Smarters availability changes more often on LG. If you own a Samsung as well, see our Samsung Smart TV guide.

LG-specific fixes that solve most problems

The app freezes or channels stop loading after a few days

LG's Quick Start+ setting keeps the TV in a low-power standby instead of fully shutting down, so apps are never really restarted. Turn it off under Settings → General → Devices/Quick Start+, or unplug the TV for 60 seconds. This clears a large share of "it worked yesterday" reports.

Picture buffers on Wi-Fi but not on your phone

Many TV Wi-Fi chips are weaker than the one in your phone. Use the Ethernet port if you can. Even TVs with 100 Mbps ports have four times the 25 Mbps a 4K stream needs. More tips in our buffering guide.

"Playlist not found" after uploading

Check that the MAC you typed online matches the one on screen character for character (0 vs O is the usual culprit), then use the app's reload or update-playlist option.

FAQ

LG Smart TV IPTV Questions

Can I install TiviMate on an LG TV? +
No. TiviMate is an Android app and LG TVs run webOS, which can't install Android apps. Use IBO Player, Smart IPTV or SS IPTV on the TV, or add a Firestick if you want TiviMate specifically.
Is IPTV Smarters Pro available on LG webOS? +
Sometimes. Smarters versions have appeared in the LG Content Store for certain models and regions, but availability changes. If you can't find it, IBO Player offers a similar layout and is free with our 1-year plan.
What is the best IPTV app for LG smart TVs? +
For most people it is IBO Player because of its layout and VOD support, followed by Smart IPTV. SS IPTV is the best free option. All three work with IPTVmojo.
Do I need to pay for the app as well as IPTVmojo? +
Smart IPTV charges a one-time activation after its trial; SS IPTV is free. IBO Player activation is included with the IPTVmojo 1-year plan, so you pay nothing extra.
Why does my LG TV show a MAC address in the IPTV app? +
MAC-based apps use it as your account ID: you upload your playlist on the app's website against that MAC, and the TV downloads it. You never type long URLs with the remote.
Does IPTV work on LG TVs in Canada? +
Yes. The apps and the setup are the same in Canada, the US and Europe. Your IPTVmojo subscription works anywhere with a stable internet connection.
