We stream in SD, HD, Full HD and up to 4K Ultra HD. Most premium sports channels and major live events broadcast in Full HD or 4K where available.

You get 50,000+ live channels spanning North American, European, and international networks, plus 300,000+ movies and TV series on demand. Every plan also includes premium sports packages (NFL, NBA, NHL, UFC, PPV, F1), an EPG guide, and Catch-up TV.

Yes. Every plan includes NFL, NBA, NHL, UFC, Boxing, Formula 1, Premier League, Champions League, La Liga, and more. PPV and major special events are included with all subscriptions at no extra charge.

For HD streaming a stable 10 Mbps connection is sufficient. For 4K content we recommend at least 25–30 Mbps. A wired Ethernet connection or strong Wi-Fi signal gives the smoothest experience during peak-time live sports.
