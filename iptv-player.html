Player vs provider: the one thing to understand

Search "IPTV player" and you'll find hundreds of apps, and none of them come with TV channels. The player is like a web browser and the provider (such as IPTVmojo) is like the website. You add the provider's login to the player, as an M3U playlist or Xtream Codes details, and the channels appear. You can switch players at any time without changing your subscription.

Can you watch IPTV in a web browser?

Sometimes, but it's the least reliable option, and here's why. Most IPTV live channels are delivered as MPEG-TS streams, which browsers can't play natively. Web players have to convert them on the fly in JavaScript. Browsers also block plain-HTTP video inside HTTPS pages ("mixed content"), and many stream servers don't send the cross-origin headers a web page needs. The result is web players that load the channel list but show a black screen.

If you only have a computer, install VLC (free on Windows, Mac and Linux), use Media → Open Network Stream and paste your M3U link. Or use IPTV Smarters for Windows, or the free MyIPTV Player from the Microsoft Store, for a proper channel guide. Both are covered in the PC & Mac guide.

How to choose an IPTV player

  1. Login type: pick a player that supports Xtream Codes. It loads channels, guide, movies and series in one go and refreshes automatically. Plain M3U works everywhere but is slower with big playlists.
  2. TV guide (EPG): essential for live sports. TiviMate, IBO Player and MyTVOnline have the best grids.
  3. Catch-up: IPTVmojo offers up to 7 days of catch-up on supported channels, but only players with catch-up support (TiviMate Premium, Smarters, MyTVOnline, IBO) can use it.
  4. Price: Smarters, VLC, OTT Navigator and SS IPTV are free. TiviMate Premium and some TV apps charge the developer directly. IBO Player is free with our 1-year plan.
  5. Official source: install from the device's app store or the developer's own site. Avoid "premium unlocked" APKs.

FAQ

IPTV Player Questions

What is the best IPTV player? +
On Firestick and Android TV it's TiviMate. On iPhone and Apple TV, IPTV Smarters. On Samsung and LG TVs, IBO Player. On computers, VLC or IPTV Smarters. The best one is whichever fits your device.
Do IPTV players come with channels? +
No. Players are empty until you add a subscription. IPTVmojo provides the channels; you choose whichever player you prefer.
What is the best free IPTV player? +
IPTV Smarters Pro is free on almost every platform. VLC is free on computers, OTT Navigator on Android and SS IPTV on Samsung/LG.
Can I use an M3U player with IPTVmojo? +
Yes. Every IPTVmojo account includes an M3U playlist link and Xtream Codes details, so any M3U or Xtream-compatible player works.
Why does my IPTV web player show a black screen? +
Browsers can't natively play the MPEG-TS format most live channels use, and they block HTTP streams inside HTTPS pages. Use VLC or a desktop app instead.
Can I use two different players with one subscription? +
Yes, as long as only as many streams play at the same time as your plan allows. A 1-connection plan means one stream at a time.
