SmartOne, SET IPTV, SS IPTV & IBO Player

These four smart-TV players work the same way. The TV shows a MAC address, and you upload your playlist to that MAC on the app's website. Once you understand that, any of them takes five minutes.

Setup

Setup in 5 Steps (Any of the Four Apps)

1

Install the app on the TV

Open your TV's app store (Samsung Apps, LG Content Store, Google Play) and install SmartOne IPTV, SET IPTV, SS IPTV or IBO Player.

2

Open it and copy the MAC

The start screen shows a MAC address like 00:1A:79:xx:xx:xx, and IBO Player also shows a device key. Take a photo of it with your phone.

3

Go to the app's official website

On your phone or computer, open the developer's site and find the playlist upload, playlist editor or manage-device page.

4

Paste your IPTVmojo M3U link

Enter the MAC (and key), give the playlist a name and paste the M3U URL from your IPTVmojo welcome email. Save.

5

Restart the TV app

Fully close the app and reopen it. It downloads the playlist, and live TV, movies and series appear.

How MAC-activated IPTV apps work

Typing a 100-character playlist URL with a TV remote is miserable, so these apps take a different approach. Each TV gets an ID, its MAC address. You attach your playlist to that ID on a website, and the TV fetches it. The app developer charges per device (where it charges at all) and the IPTV provider supplies the channels. They are separate products, so your IPTVmojo subscription and the app's activation are two different things.

Because the MAC is effectively your login, treat it like a password. Don't post screenshots of it online, and only share it with your provider when you're asking for help.

Notes for each app

IBO Player

The most polished of the group, with separate Live, Movies and Series sections, an EPG grid and favourites. On the 1-year plan the IBO Player subscription is included. Contact our WhatsApp support after ordering to claim it. You add your playlist under Manage playlists on the IBO website.

SET IPTV

Lightweight and fast on older Samsung and LG sets. Upload the M3U link on the SET IPTV site against your MAC. If you also want a separate EPG source, there is a field for it, but IPTVmojo playlists already carry guide data.

SmartOne IPTV

Searches for "SmartOne IPTV generate" usually come from people looking for the playlist step. You don't generate anything. Enter your MAC on the SmartOne website and paste your M3U link there. The app shows the playlist after a restart.

SS IPTV

SS IPTV (Simple Smart IPTV, often written SSIPTV) is free and ad-light. In the app, open Settings → General → Get code. The code is single-use and expires quickly. Enter it in the playlist editor on the SS IPTV website, add your M3U link as an external playlist, and save. SS IPTV is best for live channels; its VOD handling is basic.

Flix IPTV, Hot IPTV and Duplecast

These are also MAC-activated players, not channel providers, despite how they're often described in search results. Setup is the same five steps above: open the app, note the MAC (and key, if shown), upload your IPTVmojo playlist on the developer's website, and restart. Use the website address shown inside the app so you know you're on the official page.

Fixes for the usual problems

  • "No playlist" after uploading: re-check the MAC character by character (0/O and 1/I mix-ups are common), then fully restart the app.
  • MAC changed: resetting the TV or reinstalling some apps can produce a new MAC. Upload the playlist again to the new one.
  • Channels load but won't play: test the same login on your phone in IPTV Smarters. If it plays there, clear the TV app's data. If not, contact support.
  • Guide (EPG) is empty: give it a few minutes after first load, then use the app's EPG refresh option.
  • Buffering: see fix IPTV buffering. A wired connection solves most TV Wi-Fi issues.

FAQ

SmartOne, SET IPTV, SS IPTV & IBO Questions

Which is better: SET IPTV, SmartOne IPTV or SS IPTV? +
SS IPTV is the best free choice for live TV. SET IPTV and SmartOne are faster and handle VOD better but charge a one-time activation. IBO Player is the most complete, and it is free with the IPTVmojo 1-year plan.
Do these apps include channels? +
No. They are players only. You need an IPTV subscription such as IPTVmojo, then upload its playlist to the app.
Where do I find my TV's MAC address for SET IPTV or SmartOne? +
It appears on the app's start screen as soon as you open it. Use exactly that value, not the network MAC from your TV settings, which can differ.
Is the SS IPTV code permanent? +
No. The SS IPTV connection code is single-use and expires quickly. Generate a new one in Settings → General each time you edit your playlist.
Will IPTVmojo activate IBO Player for me? +
Yes. The 1-year plan includes an IBO Player subscription at no extra cost. Contact our WhatsApp support after ordering to claim it.
Can I use one app activation on two TVs? +
Activations are tied to a MAC address, so each TV needs its own. Watching on two TVs at once also needs a 2-connection IPTVmojo plan.
